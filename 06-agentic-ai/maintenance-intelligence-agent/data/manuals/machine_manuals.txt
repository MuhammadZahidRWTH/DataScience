=== CNC_Mill_01 MAINTENANCE MANUAL ===

MACHINE: CNC Mill 01 (Model: DMG MORI CMX 600)
MANUFACTURER: DMG MORI
INSTALLATION: 2021

TEMPERATURE SPECIFICATIONS:
- Normal operating range: 60-70°C
- Warning threshold: 75°C
- Critical threshold: 85°C
- Action: If temperature exceeds 75°C, inspect coolant flow immediately.
- Action: If temperature exceeds 85°C, emergency stop required.

VIBRATION SPECIFICATIONS:
- Normal operating range: 0.5-1.0 mm/s
- Warning threshold: 2.0 mm/s
- Critical threshold: 4.0 mm/s
- Action: Vibration above 2.0 mm/s indicates bearing wear. Schedule inspection within 24 hours.
- Action: Vibration above 4.0 mm/s requires immediate shutdown.

BEARING MAINTENANCE:
- Inspection interval: Every 500 operating hours
- Lubrication: Shell Tellus S2 M46 grease, 10-15g per bearing
- Replacement: Every 3000 operating hours or when vibration exceeds 3.0 mm/s
- Common failure mode: Insufficient lubrication causes heat buildup and spalling

COOLANT SYSTEM:
- Coolant type: Blaser Swisslube Blasocut 4000 Strong 8%
- Flow rate: 12-15 L/min at operating pressure
- Filter replacement: Every 200 hours
- Common failure: Clogged filter causes temperature spike above 80°C

=== Hydraulic_Press_02 MAINTENANCE MANUAL ===

MACHINE: Hydraulic Press 02 (Model: Schuler MSP 250)
MANUFACTURER: Schuler AG
INSTALLATION: 2020

PRESSURE SPECIFICATIONS:
- Normal operating range: 8.0-9.0 bar
- Warning threshold: 9.5 bar
- Critical threshold: 10.5 bar
- Action: Pressure above 9.5 bar indicates seal wear. Inspect seals within 48 hours.
- Action: Pressure above 10.5 bar requires immediate shutdown.

TEMPERATURE SPECIFICATIONS:
- Normal operating range: 70-85°C
- Warning threshold: 90°C
- Critical threshold: 100°C
- Action: Overheating indicates hydraulic fluid degradation or pump wear.

HYDRAULIC SYSTEM:
- Fluid type: Fuchs Renolin MR 46 VG
- Fluid change interval: Every 2000 operating hours
- Filter replacement: Every 500 hours
- Common failure: Fluid contamination causes pressure fluctuations and seal wear

SEAL MAINTENANCE:
- Inspection interval: Every 300 operating hours
- Replacement: At first sign of leakage or pressure drop below 7.5 bar
- Seal kit: Schuler OEM Part #MSP250-SK-001

=== Conveyor_Motor_03 MAINTENANCE MANUAL ===

MACHINE: Conveyor Motor 03 (Model: SEW Eurodrive R77)
MANUFACTURER: SEW Eurodrive
INSTALLATION: 2022

TEMPERATURE SPECIFICATIONS:
- Normal operating range: 40-50°C
- Warning threshold: 58°C
- Critical threshold: 65°C
- Action: Overheating indicates blocked ventilation or overload condition.
- Action: Check ambient temperature and load distribution.

VIBRATION SPECIFICATIONS:
- Normal operating range: 0.3-0.6 mm/s
- Warning threshold: 1.2 mm/s
- Critical threshold: 2.5 mm/s
- Action: Elevated vibration indicates belt misalignment or coupling wear.

GEARBOX MAINTENANCE:
- Oil type: SEW CLP 220 gear oil
- Oil change interval: Every 10,000 hours or annually
- Oil level check: Every 500 hours
- Common failure: Oil degradation causes gear wear and temperature rise

BELT AND COUPLING:
- Belt inspection: Every 1000 hours
- Tension check: Every 250 hours, target 150-180N belt tension
- Coupling alignment: Every 6 months or after any motor replacement

GENERAL MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE:
- Daily: Visual inspection, temperature check, unusual noise check
- Weekly: Lubrication levels, belt tension, filter status
- Monthly: Full sensor calibration, bearing inspection, seal check
- Annually: Full overhaul, fluid replacement, coupling alignment

FAULT CODES AND REMEDIATION:
- F001: Temperature critical — stop machine, inspect coolant/ventilation
- F002: Vibration critical — stop machine, inspect bearings/alignment
- F003: Pressure anomaly — inspect hydraulic seals and fluid level
- F004: Overload detected — reduce load, inspect drive components
- F005: Bearing wear detected — schedule replacement within 24 hours
